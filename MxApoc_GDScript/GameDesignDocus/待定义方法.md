## 9. 待澄清的歧义

### 9.1 `player.立即执行一个行动()` 的语义

**调用位置**：[SurvivorPacks/gunslinger.md](SurvivorPacks/gunslinger.md)(战术领导力)、[SurvivorPacks/surgeon.md](SurvivorPacks/surgeon.md)(注射肾上腺素)、[ScavengePacks/blue.md](ScavengePacks/blue.md)(对讲机)
